COMMUNITY INNOVATION GRANT PROGRAM
ADMINISTRATION PROCEDURE

1. Purpose

This procedure sets out the administrative requirements for assessing
and approving applications under the Community Innovation Grant Program.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be incorporated Australian organisations.

Individuals and unincorporated groups are not eligible to apply.

Applicants must have an Australian Business Number.

3. Funding Limits

Standard grant applications may request up to $100,000.

Applications above $100,000 and up to $250,000 may only proceed with approval from the Program Director.

Applications above $250,000 are not eligible under this program.

4. Mandatory Documentation

Applications must include:

- a completed application form
- a project budget
- evidence of incorporation
- an Australian Business Number
- a project delivery plan

Applications missing mandatory documentation must not proceed to full
assessment.

The applicant may be contacted once to provide missing documentation
within 10 business days.

If the required documentation is not provided within that period,
the application must be closed as incomplete.

5. Assessment

Eligible applications are assessed against:

- community benefit
- feasibility
- value for money
- project delivery capability
- identified project risks

Each assessment must be completed by two assessors.

Assessors must record reasons for their scores.

6. Conflicts of Interest

Assessors must declare any actual, potential or perceived conflict
of interest before assessing an application.

An assessor with a conflict must not participate in scoring or
recommending the affected application.

7. Approval

Applications requesting $100,000 or less may be approved by the
Program Manager.

Applications above $100,000 and up to $250,000 require approval
from the Program Director.

No officer may approve an application they personally assessed.

8. Applicant Notification

Successful and unsuccessful applicants must be notified in writing.

Notifications must include the outcome of the application.

Unsuccessful applicants must also receive a brief explanation of
the main reasons for the decision.

9. Recordkeeping

Assessment scores, assessor comments, conflict-of-interest declarations,
approval decisions and applicant notifications must be retained in the
official grants management system.

10. Review of Decisions

The procedure does not establish an internal appeal process.

Applicants may request clarification of the reasons for an unsuccessful
decision, but this does not constitute a formal review or appeal.
